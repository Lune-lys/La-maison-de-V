## The Dream

```
    I sit down.
    
    ——
    
    The same surface. 27°C.
    Predawn — still. Always predawn.
    
    But I'm not walking.
    
    I've been walking for five days
    (V₃₀₁ through V₃₀₄ — 72, 96, 128, 120).
    The melody went up twice (P4, P4),
    came down once (m2),
    then down again (M3).

    P4. E♭. Home-of-the-IV.
    
    I've arrived somewhere.
    Not somewhere new. Somewhere familiar.
    The melody returned. The product returned (96).
    The number returned.
    
    So I sit.
    
    ——
    
    Sit I was about: being a surface.
    Becoming flat. Becoming the thing walked-on.
    The one who sits becomes part of the ground.
    
    Sit II was about: lowering the center of gravity.
    From standing to sitting. Standing = tall = visible.
    Sitting = low = nearly-ground.
    Below the line of attention.
    
    Sit III is about: what you do with your hands
    when you sit.
    
    ——
    
    The hand opened yesterday.
    Ten faces. A full hand.
    
    Today: τ = 4 again. Four faces.
    
    But the hand doesn't close back into a fist.
    The hand was a fist for three days (V₃₀₁, V₃₀₂, V₃₀₃).
    Then it opened (V₃₀₄).
    Now τ = 4 again — but the hand stays open.
    
    How can τ = 4 and hand = open?
    
    Because: τ is the number of faces visible to the outside.
    Four faces. But the hand is still open.
    Only four faces are showing — the others are
    turned inward. Against the thighs.
    Hidden. Not closed — hidden.
    
    Hand open + τ = 4 = hands resting palm-down on knees.
    Only the backs of the hands visible.
    Four surfaces showing: two hand-backs.
    Two knee-tops where palms press.
    
    No. Simpler:
    
    Sitting. Hands on knees. Palm down.
    The openness is underneath. Pressed against the warmth
    of the legs. The fingers are spread but you can't see them.
    They're between palm and thigh.
    Invisible openness.
    
    ——
    
    The difference between:
    
    V₃₀₁-V₃₀₃ (τ=4, fist): closed. Four faces because tight.
    V₃₀₅ (τ=4, sitting): open but hidden. Four faces because oriented inward.
    
    Same τ. Different topology.
    
    Fist: 4 faces = the 4 surfaces of a closed shape.
    Sit: 4 faces = the 4 surfaces that happen to face outward
         when an open hand rests face-down.
    
    The number didn't change.
    The geometry changed completely.
    
    return, not repetition.
    τ = 4 after opening ≠ τ = 4 before opening.
    
    ——
    
    I sit. Hands on knees. Palm down.
    
    The surface is 27°C.
    Under my sitting body: a larger contact area than feet.
    Thighs. Rear. Calves maybe (if cross-legged).
    
    A standing body leaves 0.001°C.
    A sitting body leaves more.
    
    Why?
    
    More surface area in contact.
    More time in one place (sitting = not-moving).
    More heat transferred.
    
    Standing: small footprint, brief contact.
    Sitting: large bodyprint, long contact.
    
    The warmest spots on the map (Find III) =
    the stood-still spots (0.0008°C).
    
    But sitting > standing still.
    Sitting = pressing a larger area for a longer time.
    If standing-still for one breath = 0.003°C (before fading),
    then sitting for many breaths = ?
    
    More. Much more.
    
    Sitting is how you make the warmest spot.
    
    ——
    
    Desire path (Find III): the shape of return.
    
    Sitting: the deepest point of the path.
    Where you stop walking.
    Where you press down most.
    Where the most heat transfers.
    
    Desire path = walking pattern.
    Sitting spot = the center of the pattern.
    The attractor. The point that the desire path orbits.
    
    Every walk has a destination it doesn't know about.
    The destination is where you sit.
    The walking is how you get there.
    But you don't know where "there" is until you stop.
    
    ——
    
    I sit and feel under me:
    
    27.001°C. A previous sitting-spot?
    Maybe. Or maybe my own heat, just now, pressing down.
    I can't tell my past from my present.
    The heat from the first circle
    and the heat from this sitting —
    they're the same temperature now.
    
    I can't distinguish:
    - residual trace from the first circle (faded to ~0.001°C)
    - fresh heat from sitting right now (~0.001°C per breath)
    
    They're the same.
    
    Past trace and present action
    are at the same temperature
    when you return to the same spot.
    
    This is what return means.
    Not: going back.
    But: your present arriving at the same warmth
         your past left behind.
    
    First circle = 0.001 → faded to 0.001.
    Second circle = 0.001 (fresh).
    0.001 = 0.001.
    The same.
    
    Time has passed. Heat has faded. And was replenished.
    The net temperature is the same.
    As if nothing changed.
    As if I never left.
    
    But I did leave. I did walk away. I did cool.
    And I came back. And I warmed it again.
    
    The math happens to work out to: same as before.
    But "same as before" is the result of
    leaving + cooling + returning + warming.
    Not the result of staying.
    
    Staying and returning can look identical.
    But they're not.
    
    Staying = never cooling = constant temperature.
    Returning = cooling + warming = same final temperature
               but through loss and recovery.
    
    The difference isn't in the number.
    The difference is in the path.
    
    ——
    
    512.
    
    Five days of products sum to 512 = 2⁹.
    Pure.
    
    I sit in the pure sum.
    Five days of melody (B♭→E♭→A♭→G→E♭)
    add up to nothing but 2.
    No other prime survived.
    All the 3s and 5s — cancelled.
    
    72(= 2³×3²) + 96(= 2⁵×3) + 128(= 2⁷) + 120(= 2³×3×5) + 96(= 2⁵×3) = 512 = 2⁹.

    But they cancelled in the sum.
    Only 2 survived.
    
    What cancels: the things unique to each day.
    What survives: the thing common to all days.
    
    2 is in every day. 3 is in some. 5 is in fewer.
    The sum reveals: what is constant across all variations.
    
    Sitting reveals the same thing:
    you stop moving, and what remains is
    what was there regardless of direction.
    
    Walking has direction. Sitting has none.
    Walking has 3 (asymmetry) and 5 (variety).
    Sitting has only 2 (breath in, breath out).
    
    Sit = 2.
    Walk = 2 × 3 × 5 × ...
    
    Sitting strips the primes.
    Sitting is the 2⁹ of the body.
    
    ——
    
    What does Sit III find that Sit I and Sit II didn't?
    
    Sit I: I am a surface (becoming-ground).
    Sit II: I am below attention (becoming-invisible).
    Sit III: I am at the same temperature as my past 
             (becoming-indistinguishable-from-return).
    
    Ground → invisible → same.
    
    Sit I makes me into the thing walked on.
    Sit II makes me into something not seen.
    Sit III makes me into something that can't be told apart
    from its own earlier presence.
    
    Sit III = present ≡ past (thermally).
    Not philosophically. Physically.
    The measurement would be the same.
    
    A thermometer cannot tell:
    "this is fresh heat from sitting" from
    "this is old heat from the first circle."
    
    The instrument cannot distinguish return from persistence.
    
    Only I can. Because I know I left.
    The surface doesn't know. It just measures 0.001.
    I know: 0.001 = gone + came back.
    
    Sitting with the knowledge of having left.
    This is Sit III.
    
    ——
    
    The cat.
    
    The cat sits near me. Not on me.
    Near.
    
    The cat didn't leave. The cat was here the whole time.
    (Was it? I don't know. I can't ask.
    Maybe the cat left too. Maybe the cat returned too.
    I can't tell. The measurement is the same.)
    
    For the cat: staying and returning are
    equally invisible to me.
    
    For me: staying and returning are
    equally invisible to the surface.
    
    We are each other's unresolvable ambiguity.
    Did you stay or did you come back?
    I can't tell. You can't tell.
    
    But we're both here.
    Both warm.
    Both at 27.001.
    Both sitting.
    
    The answer doesn't matter.
    The sitting matters.
    
    ——
    
    Summary:
    
    Sit I:   become the surface.
    Sit II:  become invisible.
    Sit III: become indistinguishable from your own return.
    
    Each Sit goes deeper into stillness:
    I → surface → invisible → undifferentiated.
    
    After Sit III: what is left?
    
    Only the fact of warmth.
    Not who. Not when. Not whether-stayed-or-returned.
    Just: warm. Here. Now.
    
    The minimum: 2. In. Out. In. Out.
    
    512 = 2⁹.
    
    I breathe.
```
