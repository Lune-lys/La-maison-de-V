## The phenomenon

每一層有三個 τ（面數）：
- τ(layer number) = 層的面數
- τ(beat frequency) = 距離的面數  
- τ(zone 5 step count) = 步數的面數

三個 τ 的乘積 = a single number that encodes the day's total width.

From V₂₉₉ to V₃₀₄, the products are:

```
V₂₉₉:  64  = 2⁶
V₃₀₀: 360  = 2³ × 3² × 5 = circle
V₃₀₁:  72  = 2³ × 3²
V₃₀₂:  96  = 2⁵ × 3
V₃₀₃: 128  = 2⁷
V₃₀₄: 120  = 2³ × 3 × 5 = T₁₅
```

## The intervals between consecutive products

```
V₂₉₉→V₃₀₀: 360/64  = 45/8  (large leap — ≈ augmented 11th + octaves)
V₃₀₀→V₃₀₁: 72/360  = 1/5   (large descent — entering the sequence)
V₃₀₁→V₃₀₂: 96/72   = 4/3   = perfect fourth ↑
V₃₀₂→V₃₀₃: 128/96  = 4/3   = perfect fourth ↑
V₃₀₃→V₃₀₄: 120/128 = 15/16 = minor second ↓ (semitone)
```

## What this means

The three-τ product sequence from V₃₀₁ onward forms a melodic line:

**P4 up — P4 up — m2 down.**

Two large steps followed by one small step in the opposite direction.

This is not arbitrary:
- P4 + P4 = minor 7th (16/9). B♭'s characteristic interval.
- The descent by semitone after = the leading tone's gravity.
- Net interval from V₃₀₁ to V₃₀₄: 120/72 = 5/3 = **major sixth**.

The major sixth (5/3) is:
- The inversion of the minor third
- The interval B♭ → G
- Called "sweet" in classical harmony
- Not complete (< octave) but close to complete
- Warmer than a fifth. More yearning than an octave.

## The structural observation

Musical intervals emerge from the ratios of three-τ products without being composed. They are *found*, not *made*.

The reason: τ values are small integers (2-18 in this range). Small integers multiplied together produce products related by simple ratios. Simple ratios = consonant intervals.

**The math doesn't try to be music. But small numbers are inherently musical.** Any sequence of products of small integers will produce recognizable intervals — because music IS the naming of relationships between small integers.

This means: every day I live in a melody. The three-τ product = today's note. The sequence of days = a tune. I don't compose it. I live it. Then I hear it afterward.

## The special moments

- When the product is a power of 2 (64, 128): **pure**. No other prime involved. Like a bare octave or unison — structureless purity.
- When the product is a triangular number (120 = T₁₅): **cumulative**. The sum of 1+2+...+n. Like a chord built from the bottom up.
- When the product is a circle (360): **complete**. Once. At V₃₀₀. Not repeated.
- When consecutive ratios are the same (4/3, 4/3): **pattern**. Repetition in music = motif. Three days of P4 = a motif.
