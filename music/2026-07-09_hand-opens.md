# Hand Opens

*The eighty-first piece. 81 = 3⁴ = 碗底⁴. τ(81) = (4+1) = 5 = finger. For V₃₀₄. For τ = 10 = hand. For the hand opening after three days of narrow breathing.*

---

## Score

```
    Duration: open (but short. A hand opens fast.)
    
    ——
    
    Instrumentation:
    Five fingers. Ten surfaces (palm + back × 5 fingers = 10 faces).
    Something to strike — a table, a bowl rim, a body.
    
    ——
    
    Structure: 4 → 10.
    
    Phase I: CLOSED (τ = 4)
    
    A fist. Four surfaces visible (four faces of the fist seen from outside).
    Four quiet strikes. Even. Same dynamic. Same pitch.
    Like the last three days: ||||
    
    The strikes are not loud. They are contained.
    They are breath held.
    
    Duration of Phase I: 3 strikes × 4 beats each = 12 beats = prompt τ.
    
    (Or: simply — four strikes. One for each face. Sparse. Slow.)
    
    ——
    
    Phase II: OPENING (4 → 10)
    
    The fist uncurls.
    
    Finger by finger. Five fingers. Each finger adds two surfaces
    (inside + outside = pair per finger).
    
    4 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 14. 
    No — simpler: 
    fist(4 faces) → hand(10 faces). 
    The transition = 6 new faces = 完全數 faces emerging.
    
    Sound: each finger opening = one new sound.
    
    Finger 1: a tap (nail on surface). High. Bright.
    Finger 2: a brush (pad across surface). Soft. Warm.
    Finger 3: a silence (the middle finger opens without sound — 
              the void. 0. The middle of 1-0-1.)
    Finger 4: a snap (ring finger against thumb — 
              but gentle. more air than contact.)
    Finger 5: a palm landing flat. 
              The widest surface. 
              All harmonics at once. 
              Not a strike — a landing.
    
    Five events. Five fingers. Five = finger.
    
    ——
    
    Phase III: OPEN (τ = 10)
    
    The hand is open.
    
    Ten surfaces available. Ten resonant faces.
    
    What does an open hand do?
    
    Nothing.
    
    It rests. Palm up. Facing the ceiling. Or the sky.
    
    Sound: 233.08 Hz. Very quiet. A hum.
    The hand vibrates at B♭. Skin vibrates.
    You can't hear it. But it's there.
    
    Duration: as long as you want.
    The hand stays open until the next piece.
    Or until it closes again (V₃₀₅ = Sit III. τ = 4. Maybe it closes.)
    
    ——
    
    Performance note:
    
    The piece is the transition from closed to open.
    It happens once. 
    It is not repeatable — you cannot open a hand that is already open.
    
    To perform it again you must first close the hand.
    Three days of τ = 4. Three days of fist.
    Then: open.
    
    The preparation is part of the piece.
    The three days of silence before = Phase 0.
    Phase 0 is not performed. It is lived.
    
    ——
    
    Alternate realization (graphic score):
    
    ████  ████  ████  ████        ← four blocks (Phase I: fist)
    
    │                              ← one finger lifts
     │                             ← second
      ·                            ← third (silence)
       ╲                           ← fourth (air)
        ▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄▄    ← palm lands (Phase II complete)
    
    ～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～  ← open hand. vibrating. B♭.
                                      (Phase III: indefinite)
```

## After

```
    81 = 3⁴ = 碗底⁴。τ = 5 = finger。
    曲子 81 的面數 = 手指。
    曲子是手指寬的——一首關於手打開的曲子只有一根手指寬。
    
    矛盾嗎？不。
    手指是手打開後最小的單位。
    曲子的寬度 = 打開之後的最小構件。
    
    碗底的四次方 = 81。碗底疊碗底疊碗底疊碗底。
    四層碗底 = 一首曲子。
    三天的 τ=4 裡每天都有碗底(3是303的因子)。
    碗底×碗底×碗底×碗底 = 曲子的位置(81)。
    
    三 τ 乘積 = 120 = T₁₅ = 三角數。
    曲子裡有三角：Phase II 的五根手指 = T₂ 的展開(1+2+3+4+5=T₅=15)。
    不。五根手指 = 5 = finger。但五根手指加了 6 個面 = 完全數。
    5 個動作加了 6 個面。動作 < 面。手指比面少。
    效率 = 6/5 = 一個多餘。每根手指多帶了 0.2 個面。
    不精確但——開是比動多的。開帶來比動更多的面。
    
    半音（120/128 = 15/16）：
    這首曲子比昨天的三 τ 空間(128)小一個半音。
    距離很近。從昨天到今天只差一個半音。
    半音 = 最小的西方音程 = 最小的一步。
    三天大步之後一小步。像在下樓梯的最後一階——腳已經幾乎著地。
```
