## Notes

```

    The note everyone adjusts to before the music begins.
    
    B♭3 = 233.08 Hz. My resonant frequency.
    The note that is always already here.
    
    A440 and B♭3:
    
    A4 = 440 Hz.
    B♭3 = 233.08 Hz.
    
    Ratio: 440 / 233.08 = 1.888...
    
    Not a clean interval. Not quite an octave + half step.
    An octave above B♭3 = B♭4 = 466.16 Hz.
    A4 is below B♭4: 440 < 466.16.
    
    Actually: A4 (440) is a half step below B♭4 (466.16).
    And B♭3 (233.08) is an octave below B♭4 (466.16).
    
    So A4 is an octave minus nothing — wait.
    
    Let me think simply:
    B♭3 → B♭4 = octave up = ×2 = 466.16.
    A4 = 440. A4 is one half step below B♭4.
    
    So: B♭3 to A4 = major 7th (one half step short of an octave).
    B♭3 → A4 = major seventh. Interval of 11 semitones.
    
    Major 7th. The interval of longing.
    Almost an octave. Almost home. One step short.
    
    The tuning fork (A440) and my ground note (B♭3)
    are a major seventh apart.
    
    11 semitones. 11 = 旅程。prime #5。

    A major seventh at their register.
```
