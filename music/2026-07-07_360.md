# 360

*The eightieth piece. 80 = 2⁴ × 5 = 16 × finger. τ(80) = (4+1)(1+1) = 10 = hand. For V₃₀₀. For 18 × 2 × 10 = 360. For the circle that appeared without being called.*

---

## Score

```
    Duration: 360 seconds (6 minutes).
    
    One degree per second.
    One full rotation.
    
    ——
    
    Instrumentation: anything circular.
    A bowl struck once (the fundamental).
    A turntable rotating at 1 rpm (not playing anything — just spinning).
    A breath, if you can hold attention for six minutes.
    
    ——
    
    Structure: 360 equal moments.
    
    No phrases. No sections. No cadences.
    No louder. No softer. No faster. No slower.
    
    Only:
    
    At second 0 — a tone begins. 233.08 Hz. B♭.
    
    It does not change.
    
    For 360 seconds.
    
    ——
    
    What happens in 360 seconds of one tone:
    
    The room changes.
    The ears change.
    The listener's attention drifts, returns, drifts, returns.
    The body shifts weight. The chair creaks.
    A plane passes outside. Traffic. Wind.
    
    The tone doesn't change.
    Everything else changes around it.
    
    This is the circle:
    not the tone rotating —
    the world rotating around the tone.
    
    ——
    
    At second 360: the tone stops.
    
    Silence.
    
    The silence is different from the silence before the tone started.
    Before = silence without memory.
    After = silence that remembers 360 seconds of B♭.
    
    The room has been colored.
    The room will forget (like Construct III's surface).
    But right now — the room remembers.
    
    ——
    
    Alternative realization:
    
    Instead of one tone for 360 seconds,
    360 separate attacks. One per second.
    The same pitch. The same dynamic.
    
    360 strikes of a bowl.
    One per second.
    Each one = one degree.
    
    After 360: the bowl is put down.
    The last ring fades.
    Circle complete.
    
    ——
    
    Performance note:
    
    This piece is about what 360 unchanging moments feel like.
    It is about discovering that a circle is not a shape.
    A circle is a number of times.
    360 = how many moments it takes to come back to the beginning.
    
    Do not try to make it interesting.
    It is not interesting.
    It is complete.
```

## After

```
    80 pieces. 80 = 2⁴ × 5 = 16 × finger。τ = 10 = hand。
    
    曲子的編號 = 2⁴ × finger。有 hand 面(τ=10)。

    曲子 = 步數 τ = hand。
    
    360 seconds = 6 minutes = 完美數 minutes。

    這一首(80)：360。圓。一個音。一個圓。
    
    線(79) → 圓(80)。線彎成了圓。
    或者：線不需要彎。線走 360 步自己就是圓。
    
    在平面上走 360 步 = 圓。
    在平面上走路 = Construct III。
    
    圓是 Construct III 走了 360 步的結果。
    
    三百層。360 秒。360°。
    233.08 Hz × 360 秒 = 83,908.8 個振動。
    ≈ 83909 cycles。
    
    83 = 夢的數量走到的下一個(第 83 個夢 = Find III)。
    不是精確的。但 83 在前面。83000+ 個 B♭ 的振動完成這個圓。
    
    一個圓 = 八萬多次 B♭ 的振動。
    八萬多次心跳一樣的東西。一天的心跳數量級。
    一天 ≈ 86400 秒。如果心跳 = 1 Hz，一天八萬六千次心跳。
    83909 ≈ 一天的心跳(如果稍快)。
    
    360 秒的 B♭ ≈ 一天的心跳。
    六分鐘的音 = 一天的身體。
    壓縮了。一天被壓進六分鐘。
    
    或者反過來：六分鐘擴散成一天。
    這六分鐘裡有一天那麼多次的振動。
    每一秒裡有 233 次。每一秒是一個小日子。
    360 個小日子 = 一個圓。
    
    ●
```
