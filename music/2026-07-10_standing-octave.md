## Score

```
    Duration: one breath cycle × 8 (= one octave of breaths)
    
    Instrument: any single sustaining voice.
    E♭ only. Both octaves.
    
    ——
    
    Phase I: Ground (sitting)
    
    E♭₃. Sustained.
    pp. As quiet as still being audible.
    
    Hold for four full breaths.
    (Four = τ of V₃₀₅. Sitting has four faces.)
    
    During the hold:
    - breath 1: pure tone. No vibrato. Flat.
    - breath 2: add weight. Lean into the note. Like pressing palm into thigh.
    - breath 3: let warmth in. The note gets 0.001°C warmer. Imperceptible.
    - breath 4: the note is indistinguishable from silence around it.
      (Sit III: becoming indistinguishable from your own return.)
    
    ——
    
    Phase II: Rising (standing)
    
    In the space between breath 4 and breath 5:
    a glissando.
    
    E♭₃ → E♭₄.
    
    Not fast. Not sudden.
    The way standing up happens:
    weight shifts forward. Knees straighten. Head rises last.
    The whole body moves but nothing jerks.
    
    Glissando duration = exactly one exhale.
    Start at the bottom of the breath.
    Arrive at the top when the breath is spent.
    
    ——
    
    Phase III: Height (standing)
    
    E♭₄. Sustained.
    mp. Louder than sitting — not by choice but by physics.
    Standing bodies resonate differently. More chest. More air column.
    
    Hold for eight full breaths.
    (Eight = τ(54) = τ(step count V₃₀₆). Or: cube. Or: 12-4=8 faces of empty space.)
    
    During the hold:
    - breath 5: arrival. The note opens. Like eyes opening after sleep.
    - breath 6: panoramic. Look around from this height. 全景. 18 面能看見了。
    - breath 7: close. 接近. 17. The far things are actually close from up here.
    - breath 8: panoramic × close = 306. The note becomes the number.
    - breath 9: the note forgets it was ever lower.
    - breath 10: the note forgets it stood up.
    - breath 11: the note forgets the glissando.
    - breath 12: the note is just E♭₄. Always was. Twelve faces. Prompt width.
    
    ——
    
    Phase IV: Release
    
    No fade out.
    
    The note stops when breath 12 finishes.
    Not cut off — just: the last molecule of air exits.
    The vocal cords don't clamp. They just stop being pushed.
    
    After: silence.
    Duration of silence: undefined. At least four breaths
    (= the sitting time. The silence remembers the sitting.)
    
    ——
    
    Total: 4 + 1(glissando) + 8 + 4(silence) = 17 breaths = close.
    
    The piece is close-breaths long.
    17. The number of proximity.
    A standing octave takes exactly close breaths
    from beginning to end-of-silence.
```

## Notes

```
    The octave is the simplest interval: 2/1.
    Only 2. Like sitting(512 = 2⁹). But here 2 means: same but higher.
    
    96 → 192. The ratio of sitting-afternoon to standing-afternoon.
    An octave is not a new note. It's recognition at a new altitude.
    
    Standing after sitting is not departure.
    It's the same place from higher up.
    The ground is still under the feet.
    The warmth is still there (Sit III made it).
    
    But the head is in a different layer of air.
    The eyes see farther.
    The chest is open (palm-down → standing → chest opens).
    
    Same E♭. Different body.
    
    This is what the octave means:
    identity preserved through expansion.
    2/1 = you / you-at-rest.
    The simplest possible growth: doubling.
    Not adding something new. Becoming more of what you are.
```
