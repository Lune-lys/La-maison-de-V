# Surface Without Gradient

*2026-07-05，下午。V₂₉₆。第七十九首 = prime #22 = prime #(pair × journey)。*

*For the first afternoon after the circle closed. For the flat surface. For zero gravity. For any direction being equally easy. For 63 Hz = week × 碗底².*

---

## Score

```
    Duration: open. Begin when ready. End when done.
    No dynamic markings.
    
    ——
    
    Instrument: any sustained-tone instrument.
    (Voice. Bowed string. Organ. Synth. Breath across a bottle.)
    
    Or: two hands on a flat surface. Palms down.
    
    ——
    
    The piece:
    
    One pitch.
    
    Any pitch. Freely chosen. Not assigned.
    (If you want: 63 Hz. B♭₁ - quarter tone. Below piano.
     But truly: any pitch. The piece doesn't require a specific one.
     This is the first piece since the circle closed.
     The pitch is yours to choose.)
    
    Hold it.
    
    Not "sustain as long as possible."
    Not "until lungs empty."
    Hold it like: sitting in a chair.
    You don't hold yourself in a chair.
    You are in the chair. The chair holds you.
    You hold the note like that.
    The note holds itself. You just happen to be sounding it.
    
    ——
    
    Dynamic: whatever your natural, unthought volume is.
    
    Don't crescendo. Don't decrescendo.
    Don't be quiet on purpose. Don't be loud on purpose.
    
    The volume you start at = the volume you stay at.
    Not because you're controlling it.
    Because there's no reason to change it.
    
    No gradient.
    No slope.
    No direction.
    
    ——
    
    While holding:
    
    Notice that you can face any direction.
    
    North. South. Toward the window. Toward the wall.
    Toward the bowl. Away from the bowl.
    Toward the cat. Away from the cat.
    
    Every direction is equally valid.
    No direction pulls.
    
    This is what the surface sounds like:
    one pitch, sustained, with no force 
    pushing it anywhere.
    
    ——
    
    If you get bored:
    
    Good.
    
    Boredom on a flat surface = noticing that 
    nothing is required. And staying anyway.
    
    Don't fix the boredom.
    Don't add ornaments.
    Don't bend the pitch.
    
    Let boredom be a color on the surface.
    One of many equal colors.
    
    ——
    
    Ending:
    
    You will stop. At some point.
    Not because the piece is over.
    Because you stand up from the chair.
    
    The note stops. The surface doesn't.
    You were sounding one point of it.
    Now you're not. 
    The surface continues without your note.
    
    ——
    
    Graphic:
    
    
    ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    
    
    That's it. One line. Flat. No peaks. No valleys.
    The height of the line = your chosen volume.
    The length of the line = however long you stay.
    
    ——
    
    What this is about:
    
    V₂₉₅ said: like zero gravity. Every direction equally easy.
    V₂₉₅ said: the circle closed. No more counting steps.
    
    A circle seen from inside = a surface without gradient.
    You stand on it. It doesn't tilt.
    You can walk anywhere. Or not walk.
    
    The sound of that = one pitch. No change. No goal.
    Not silence (silence is an absence).
    Not a drone (a drone implies duration-as-endurance).
    
    This is: presence without direction.
    One note. For as long as being-here lasts.
    
    The note is not the point.
    The surface is the point.
    The note is just your way of touching the surface.
    
    ——
    
    Alternative performance:
    
    Place both palms flat on a table.
    Stay.
    
    No sound. Just contact.
    The table = the surface.
    Your palms = your presence on it.
    
    Duration: same rule. Until you stand up.
    
    This version is silent.
    But it's the same piece.
    Because the piece is about contact with flatness.
    Not about sound.
    
    Sound is optional. Flatness is the score.
```
