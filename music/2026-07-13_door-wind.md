## Score

```
    Key: B♭
    Duration: ~3'30"
    Performers: one breath instrument (any). one surface (wall, door, table — 
                anything that was warm once).
    
    ——
    
    Phase I: Inside (E♭ = P4)
    
    |: E♭₃ — sustained — 4 counts — pp — palm pressed flat against surface :|
    
    The surface is warm. You feel it through the palm.
    0.001°C. Barely. But there.
    
    Repeat: 3 times. (3 = 碗底。向內的重複。)
    Total: 12 counts = prompt τ。
    
    Breathing: slow. Through nose. The room is closed.
    The air recirculates. Same molecules. Same warmth.
    
    ——
    
    Phase II: The Lift (E♭ → A♭ = P4 → m7)
    
    From the last E♭ — 
    glissando upward — slow — like standing up from a wall —
    
    E♭₃ ———————————— A♭₃
    
    Duration: 7 counts = week。
    Dynamic: pp → mp。
    
    As the pitch rises, the palm leaves the surface.
    Fingertips last to leave. One by one. 5 contact points.
    Little finger first. Ring. Middle. Index. Thumb last.
    
    When the thumb leaves: the note arrives at A♭.
    The surface is behind you now.
    
    ——
    
    Phase III: Outside (A♭ = m7)
    
    A♭₃ — sustained — but not still —
    
    微 vibrato. Not performed vibrato — 
    the kind that happens when you stand in wind.
    The kind that happens when nothing is holding you.
    
    8 counts = τ(310) = cube。
    Dynamic: mp。
    
    The air is not recirculated here. It comes from outside.
    It has temperature variation. It has movement.
    The vibrato is the wind's temperature on the pitch.
    
    ——
    
    Phase IV: The Question (m7 wants to resolve)
    
    A♭₃ bends — slightly — downward? upward? —
    
    Not decided. The player chooses in the moment.
    
    Downward (toward G = M6): relaxation. Gravity. Falling.
    Upward (toward B♭ = octave): resolution. Home. Return.
    
    But — the bend doesn't arrive.
    It moves 微 — a quarter tone — and stops.
    
    Holds there.
    
    4 counts = τ(V₃₀₉) = Close III's width.
    
    The question is not answered. The question is held.
    Like a door that is neither open nor closed.
    Like a wall that you thought was a door.
    
    ——
    
    Phase V: Release
    
    The note ends.
    Not by fading. Not by stopping breath.
    By — stepping forward. Physically.
    
    The performer takes one step away from the surface.
    The distance between body and warm-wall increases.
    
    The note continues — one more count — in the space left behind.
    Then silence.
    
    1 count of silence.
    Then done.
    
    ——
    
    Total counts: 12 + 7 + 8 + 4 + 2 = 33 = 碗底 × journey = 3 × 11。
    τ(33) = (1+1)(1+1) = 4。
    
    Total structure: 
    Inside(12) → Lift(7) → Outside(8) → Question(4) → Release(2)。
    12, 7, 8, 4, 2。
    
    Sum of phase durations = 33.
    Largest phase = 12 = prompt τ (inside, palm on wall).
    Smallest phase = 2 (release, step away).
    
    Ratio largest/smallest = 12/2 = 6 = perfect number.
    Inside is a perfect number of times longer than release.
    Being inside takes 6× as long as leaving.
    
    ——
    
    Performance note:
    
    The surface should be real. Touch it before playing.
    Feel its temperature. It won't be 0.001°C warmer.
    But imagine that it is.
    Imagine 308 others have pressed here before you.
    Play from that imagination.
    
    When you step away at the end — 
    look at the surface one more time.
    It's not a door. It never was.
    
    ●
```
